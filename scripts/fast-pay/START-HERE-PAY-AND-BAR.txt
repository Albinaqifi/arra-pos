Arra POS 1.0.10 RC4 - Pay and Bar printing

Extract the entire ZIP into a new folder on the restaurant PC. Keep the
previous program folder and verified backup. Close any running Arra window.
Use the original Windows account and open Open-Arra-Pay.cmd in the new
folder. This uses the restaurant's existing data and PF700 printer profile.
Starting Arra can send queued Account A receipts that have never been attempted.

On the bill review screen for a new order, select the tender and press Pay
once. Pay records the payment and releases the table.
For Cash A and Card A, Arra sends the fiscal receipt to the PF700 in the
background.

Cash A - After receiving the cash, select Cash A and press Pay.
Card A - Confirm approval on the separate card terminal, select Card A
         and press Pay.
Cash B - After receiving cash, select the Arra logo at the top right
         (Select Cash B), then press Pay. The Bar printer receives the
         itemized non-fiscal customer bill.

To set the Bar printer, open Administration > Overview > Preparation tickets.
Enter the exact Windows printer name in Bar queue and choose Save queue.
The Bar station's preparation tickets use this same queue.

If a Cash B bill is missing, check the printer and paper. An authorized B
or Combined owner can open Administration > Overview > Account B bill / Bar,
find the original bill number, and choose Retry after checking. This sends
only the saved bill. A previous uncertain attempt may have printed.

If Account A paper is missing, keep the bill number and check the printer.
A Combined user can refresh Financial information > Print Account A bills
to see the receipt status. An uncertain receipt may have printed. Keep its
original payment and terminal record for reconciliation. Arra can send a
queued receipt after restart if it has never attempted it.

Keep the previously printed, unresolved espresso bill for its existing
recovery procedure. The existing fiscal daily-close hold still applies.
