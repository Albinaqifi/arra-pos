Arra original espresso recovery check

Use the Windows account that ran the original espresso recovery. Close Arra
and its recovery tools. Extract this entire ZIP into its own folder and
double-click Check-Original-Recovery.cmd. You can use it with the printer
disconnected. Share only the single result line shown on screen.

The check reads the original local records. A MATCH means the original bill,
receipt evidence and Cash A payment agree across both files. The recovery
journal has four stages:

1. The finisher had matched the open unpaid totals and saved its intent to
   tender cash. At that point cash acceptance was still pending.
2. Before requesting closure it had checked the cash response and read back
   one item with its full amount tendered.
3. It had received the printer's close acknowledgement with its counters.
4. It had checked the closed, paid state and the next document number.

These are historical checks on one supervised sale. The files contain no
electronic-journal text from the open receipt. If the result is MISSING,
INCOMPLETE or INCONCLUSIVE, preserve the files and report that result.
You can leave the business day open.
