Arra POS 1.0.10 RC3 - Cash B customer bills on the bar printer

Extract this ZIP into its own new folder. Keep the previous RC2 folder
and verified backup. Close the previous Arra session. Use the same Windows
account and start Arra-Cash-A.cmd from the new folder for routine checkout.

In Administration > Overview > Preparation tickets, check the Bar queue.
If needed, enter the exact Windows queue name for your bar printer and
choose Save queue. The bar station and Cash B bills use this same setting.

After receiving cash, generate the bill, select the Arra logo at the top
right for Cash B, and choose Confirm bill once. This records the payment,
releases the table, and sends the itemized non-fiscal customer bill to Bar.

If the paper is missing, keep the bill number and check the printer first.
An authorized owner can open Administration > Overview, find the bill in
Account B bill, and use Retry after checking. This retries the paper only.
An uncertain earlier attempt may already have printed a copy.

The restaurant's RC2 launcher and recovery notes follow below. Read
READ-BEFORE-USE.txt for the existing Cash A and supervised Card A procedures.
