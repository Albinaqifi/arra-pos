Recover an existing paid Card A bill

Use the enrolled Windows account and the original PF700 on COM3. Keep the
bank approval and paper. Close Arra and other programs using the printer.
Leave the fiscal printer powered on. Extract all files into a new folder,
separate from the Arra installation.

1. Run Review-Receipt.cmd. Sign in locally with the Combined administrator
   username and password. It checks the existing local payment and printer
   state. The printer inquiries can replace the last cached reply.
   Review cannot register items, tender the bill or close the receipt.
2. If the review reports STOP, save that line and leave the receipt as it is.
   Do not select Pay again or use a cash recovery tool.
3. The separately authorized Finish operation asks you to verify the displayed
   items, amount, successful terminal payment and uninterrupted custody of
   the original receipt before entering its fiscal card tender. It can print
   items and close that existing document. It never starts a second receipt,
   charges a bank terminal or creates a second Arra payment. If interrupted,
   preserve both the restaurant database and the local commissioning ledger.
   An attempted device write cannot be retried automatically.
4. After confirmed completion, compare the paper with the original bill and
   save its fiscal document number. Existing unrelated fiscal holds remain.
