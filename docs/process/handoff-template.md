# Story handoff template

## Story and state
Story ID, title, status, and baseline/revision or changed-file scope.

## Builder
What changed; acceptance evidence; commands and results; limitations.

## Reviewer
Reviewed files/revision; Approved or Changes required; actionable findings and resolution.

## Tester
Each acceptance criterion and its evidence; commands and results; findings and rechecks; anything not verified.

## Next action
What remains, or Done. Note if independent roles, CI or hardware checks were unavailable. Never include secrets or real personal data.
