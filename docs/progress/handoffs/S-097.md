**Supervised trial.** The dedicated Windows profile uses the existing enrolled printer and the saved tax and product-origin settings. The waiter prepares the bill before using the separate terminal. Arra stores the observed approval and requires another confirmation to register the fiscal receipt. The printer session checks its tender registers before opening and after closing. The debit register must increase by the full bill amount, the other three tender totals and fiscal day must agree, and the next receipt and document counters must advance once. If the printer response is missing or differs, the original bill stays held. Following a crash, a verified fiscal result can finish the local payment.

A device-bound claim, terminal outcome and fiscal dispatch are recorded outside the restorable database in the commissioning Windows account. The launcher checks the original commissioned data folder and the marker left by the earlier supervised cash trial. Disagreement between these records and the database holds payment and closing. The trial allows a single terminal invitation. A confirmed decline consumes the trial and releases the unpaid bill. No customer card data is collected. The first live check must compare the approved amount, actual PF700 card receipt and one recorded Card A payment.

### Earlier simulated checkout

The sample training flow offers decline before preparing a terminal intent. Migration 48 durably holds the bill and Card A selection before the simulated terminal result, then records observed approval before fiscal preflight. If a response is lost before delivery or the app restarts, the intent stays held; a later operator can record an observed approval, but cannot release the hold as a decline. Unresolved intents and approvals hold period closing. The separate training printer records the exact card receipt and a random attempt identity. A read-only training check can recover a matching issued receipt after a lost reply without another sale; absent, older, or mismatched evidence stays uncertain. A saved acknowledgement can finish its local payment. The original bill amount, VAT groups, card tender and Account A totals are tested. This is simulated evidence only; it cannot recover a physical PF700 receipt or prove a missing receipt never began.
