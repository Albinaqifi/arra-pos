## 1.0.7 daily report

**Financial information → Close Account A** uses the selected, already closed Arra business day. The amounts on screen come from Arra. In the supervised fiscal profile, **Review official Z report** opens a separate confirmation. Confirming asks the PF700 to issue report 69 with option `0`. The printer totals the receipts already in its own fiscal registers. The close sends no bill lines or payment commands.

Arra checks each payment against its registered receipt and compares the printer identity, receipt state, date, fiscal record, last document, daily receipt count, cash, card and VAT groups with the selected day. Empty days, uncovered corrections, open receipts, activity from a different day and mismatched registers prevent issuance. A local Account A summary from a previous version keeps its print history and can acquire fiscal confirmation if its original receipts match.

Arra saves the pre-close evidence before sending a Z. A lost reply blocks another close. **Check PF700 report status (read only)** records an existing Z only after the printer proves its number, date, VAT totals, reset registers and document number. If the printer proves that the original receipts, totals and document are still open in the same fiscal period, Arra permits a newly reviewed attempt. Ambiguous evidence keeps the operation unresolved. Daily-register layouts, amount units, counter resets and physical paper output remain unverified on the installed 1.00MK firmware. In particular Arra requires command 65 to match the stored gross amounts for each tax group before it can issue a Z.

## 1.0.5 recognizes the verified printer identity

The operator's read-only comparison on COM3 showed one exact label match in field six. The firmware field identified 1.00MK dated 23 October 2014. The first field was masked. Its character pattern was visible, but its literal text was not established.

The identity check and supervised checkout now share a parser for this six-field reply. It checks the field lengths and character patterns, the firmware/date, a valid 24-hour time, four hexadecimal checksum characters, eight switch bits, one country digit and the exact supplied serial in its designated position. It also checks the frame checksum, sequence, command and device status on every request. The existing five-field format remains supported.
