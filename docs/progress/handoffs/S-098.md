# S-098 — Print bar and kitchen tickets during fiscal checkout

**In progress.** Independent review approved the offline scope and independent testing passed. Implementation `e5b1113` on `codex/s-098-preparation-fiscal-checkout` is based on `33f76ba` (PR #149). Merge and the restaurant paper check remain open.

Routine PF700 checkout now sends preparation tickets and cancellation notices to their configured Windows queues. The offline launcher gives the bar and both kitchens separate simulated queues with selectable outcomes. Both modes use the same durable attempt, status and checked-retry path. Simulated tickets cannot be replayed through Windows. The waiter can inspect each station and retry individual tickets or cancellations. Order history retains the station, adapter and outcome of each attempt.

The automated desktop-startup rehearsal opened a table, sent a mixed round and a later drink, then cancelled one kitchen item after changing its menu route. The kitchen accepted its ticket while the bar failed. Later bar and cancellation attempts had uncertain replies. No fiscal sale existed before checkout. After an interrupted simulated Cash A checkout and restart, the PF700 simulator retained one receipt for 390 MKD and Arra recorded one matching payment. Checkout recovery preserved all ticket counts. Checked retries touched only their selected jobs. A Windows print double verified the configured bar and second kitchen routes, and supervised commissioning produced no Windows output.
