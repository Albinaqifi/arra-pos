# S-102 — Cash B bills on the Bar printer

**RC4 is the combined restaurant package.** The owner selected the S-100/S-101 Pay workflow for Cash A and Card A together with S-102 Bar printing. Start `Open-Arra-Pay.cmd` under the enrolled Windows account. For a new bill, select Cash A or Card A, or select the Arra logo for Cash B, then press Pay. All three record the payment locally and release the table. A sends an itemized fiscal receipt to the PF700 in the background. B sends an itemized non-fiscal customer bill to the configured Windows Bar queue. Card charging takes place on the separate terminal.

The Cash B print attempt is reserved in the payment transaction. A B or Combined owner can inspect it under **Administration → Overview → Account B bill · Bar** and retry after checking the paper. Payment replay and restart do not automatically repeat an attempted bill. Existing fiscal holds and earlier unresolved bills retain their recovery procedures.
