# S-102 — Cash B bills on the Bar printer

In progress. The release starts from the restaurant's 1.0.10 Reader RC2 ZIP. The story branch starts at `1e8ea28`. RC3 uses the same routine launcher and existing restaurant profile.

Confirming a new Cash B payment in routine checkout reserves a bill print attempt in the payment transaction. After commit, Windows receives an itemized non-fiscal customer bill on the configured Bar queue. The print state survives restart. An owner with B or Combined access can inspect the bill under **Administration → Overview → Account B bill · Bar** and retry after checking the paper. A missing print leaves the payment recorded and the table released.
