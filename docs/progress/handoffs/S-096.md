## What a waiter can finish

Reopening the bill reads local records. The waiter can choose **Finish recording payment** when Arra already has the verified closed document acknowledgement. For a newly journaled Cash A sale on the enrolled on-site firmware, the same action is available if the app lost the final result but saved every validated reply through the final document number. The check compares the original bill and device, every item request, the cash tender, close counters and document number. It requires the complete command sequence. Bill review leaves the database untouched.

Finishing uses the original document number. Arra records one Cash A payment under the saved payer and audits the waiter who finishes it. It can finish after restarting without the printer profile. The action never sends a printer command or dispatches a Windows slip. Existing order ownership still applies. Synthetic history exercises distinct payer and finisher IDs. Current waiter entry has no cross-waiter handoff for an occupied order.

An acknowledged command to open, tender or close alone is insufficient. A lost reply, mismatching bill, partial trace or unjournaled historical attempt without a verified result stays unresolved. A prepared checkout in the current database can be released before dispatch. An older backup can predate a sale that the printer has already recorded. A missing command in that backup cannot establish that the sale never started.

## Restaurant evidence

The operator reconciled the original espresso incident. An earlier read of a reconciled closed document found the supplied item phrase. The owner also completed two hands-on training recoveries on the POS PC using the simulated printer.

The v5 USB diagnostic compares complete item, total and Cyrillic cash lines locally with the paper from a reconciled last receipt. It reports counts, the end marker and occurrences of the document number. It never accesses Arra's database. Its inquiries can overwrite the printer's last command reply. Keep it closed if any sale or fiscal report is unresolved or the last document cannot be identified.

On the POS PC the owner confirmed the last receipt was completed and recorded in Arra, with the business day still open. Two v5 reads of the same closed document each returned 32 text lines and ended at the document separator. On both reads the complete total and cash lines matched once each, and the document-number token appeared on one line. The first item entry ended in Latin B and matched zero lines. When the operator repeated the check with Cyrillic В, the complete item line matched once. These observations establish the item, total and tender text on this closed receipt. The separate number token's field is still unidentified. Neither read issued a print or sale command.

**Remaining work.** The closed-paper comparison is finished. The next physical evidence must show how to identify the saved bill and distinguish an open unpaid receipt, an open receipt already tendered in cash, and a closed receipt whose final reply is missing. The checker used here refuses open receipts. Any controlled hardware exercise needs to preserve the last reply; the operator previously deferred new real fiscal sales. Where the original sale cannot be proved never to have started, keep the hold. Once the usable evidence is established, implement and verify the supported waiter actions through restart and replay, with one fiscal receipt and one local payment. For now the finished checker can be closed. The business day may stay open.

**How to obtain that evidence.** Rehearse a supervised checkout offline with pauses after item registration, cash acceptance and closure. Preserve the acknowledged command and its validated reply locally before any inquiry at each pause. Those captured stages establish what actually happened. Separately check which document, item and tender evidence a restarted Arra could still obtain if the original reply were missing. A saved reply used as ground truth cannot also stand in for the missing evidence in that check. Use the real printer only after the procedure has a safe way to stop and the owner authorizes the possible fiscal sale and paper output. If two states still look identical to Arra, the bill stays on hold.
