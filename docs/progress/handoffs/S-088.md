# S-088 — Optional restaurant shifts

**State:** In progress; review and focused verification passed, PR #126 awaiting merge. Base: `1e7f08c86c7fe16840b115f094fe538f8656c9f8` (includes S-087); implementation commit: `c870c860588ac9f0f458c98dd1f328de5b521be3`.

Local services now track first service, scoped shift summaries, immutable closes and payment membership in the existing business day. Handover creates an idle successor; day close finalizes a reportable shift transactionally. Upgrade days with earlier or ambiguous activity retain day reporting and defer shift coverage. Payment corrections leave prior shift snapshots unchanged while day reports use effective accounts. Replays reconcile the original shift and do not rotate a successor.
