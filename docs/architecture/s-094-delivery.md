# S-094 — safe delivery slices

Status: proposed implementation plan; split billing is not enabled by this document. The S-094 acceptance criteria remain the release gate. The approved intent is unchanged; the owner direction recorded in the story supersedes its earlier no-split wording.

## Current boundaries

- Migration 20 in `src/desktop/database.js` makes `bills.order_id` unique and freezes item inserts, updates, deletes, corrections and table moves once a bill exists. Bills and their lines are immutable. Migration 21 gives each bill at most one full payment. `bills.js` finds a single existing bill by order and fingerprints all eligible sent items; `payments.js` completes the whole order on payment. These assumptions must change together before an actual split can be issued.
- Sent corrections in `order-items.js` and `admin-sent-correction.js` also reject any order with a bill. An item has one current `order_id`, and existing move and correction history reference its identity. A partially billed item cannot simply be moved or copied without accounting for its already allocated units and historical references.
- The previous `activity-report.js` restricted-owner decision read one bill row per order; the foundation change checks every effective payment instead. `order-history.js` traverses moved-order relationships and rejects orders with an outside-scope payment. Payment-based financial, day, shift, bill-history and operational reports need reconciliation against several bills and payments for one order. Existing report snapshots and print jobs remain immutable.
- `BillingBoundary` and `DesktopBill` own the billing dialog, order-write lease, uncertainty and handoff. The current success path always hands off; a split needs an authorized refresh of the still-open order and a safe final-payment handoff. Session transitions must not discard an unresolved payment identity.
- S-023 and S-024 are Done. S-067 and S-083 are In progress with rendered/offline and printer evidence outstanding. S-093 ownership is merged but its entry follow-up and desktop verification remain in progress. Build on the actual merged ownership enforcement; none of these outstanding checks is evidence for S-094.

## Invariants and transaction boundaries

For each sent item, eligible quantity is its recorded quantity less valid corrections. Allocated quantity is the sum of immutable bill-line quantities, whether their bills are unpaid or paid. Available quantity is eligible less allocated; all quantities and money must be nonnegative safe integers. A bill commits a nonempty selection and snapshots name, unit price, modifiers, quantities and total. It cannot be cancelled or reallocated. A payment changes the bill from reserved to paid but does not free its units. Pending items remain subject to the current send-or-remove billing warning.

Review is read-only. Generate must validate ownership, selection and a fresh fingerprint, then allocate and write the bill, lines and audit in one write transaction. Concurrent/stale requests cannot exceed available units; exact request replay returns the original result and a reused identity with another payload is rejected. Payment records one full amount per bill and preserves card A, cash A/B, payment day and shift. Only complete an order in that same transaction when no pending items, available sent units or unpaid reservations remain. A print failure or unknown result never reverses a payment. Reconcile an uncertain payment against the original bill and original selection before any further attempt, including after navigation, identity change or restart; never repeat the external terminal payment automatically.

Corrections may affect only available units, never reserved or paid units. Moves and additions must preserve these quantities, ownership and historical snapshots. The implementation must specify and test how available units of a partially allocated item move without rewriting the identity or quantity of already billed units; a safe lineage/quantity representation is required before enabling those moves. Do not infer an account for an unpaid bill. Restrict paid details by effective account on every direct request and connected history, including mixed A/B payments on one order. If the financial scope cannot safely project an order, fail closed.

## Ordered slices

1. **Activity privacy foundation, no new flow.** For restricted activity reports, examine every effective payment on every connected order instead of an arbitrary single bill. Preserve existing shared-unpaid behavior, Combined access, correction handling, report bounds and direct authorization. Use an isolated test schema to exercise multiple bills in both insertion orders, unpaid plus other-account payment, corrected B-to-A, connected orders and Combined without relaxing the production schema. Existing activity and visibility regressions must pass. This slice changes no schema, payment or renderer path and does not expose split billing. It is independently deployable only after review and focused testing.
2. **Remaining privacy, reporting and calculation preparation.** Audit all other order-to-bill reads and remove single-row assumptions without enabling multiple bills. Ensure unpaid information does not reveal paid details. Establish payment-ID-based reconciliation for financial, operational, day, shift and historical snapshots, including correction-effective accounts and report terminology where a payment is counted rather than an order. Exercise mixed-account/multiple-bill fixtures in isolated tests without relaxing the production schema. Preserve existing report behavior and snapshots. Independently review direct-request access and regression evidence. Extract a trusted quantity/snapshot calculation accepting recorded sent, corrected and allocated totals, while preserving the existing full-bill fingerprint, errors and IPC contract. Test corrections, partial availability, over-allocation, invalid or overflow values, modifiers and full-bill regression. No split action is exposed.
3. **Storage and service integration, gated as a unit.** Design the append-only allocation representation and safe item-move lineage with source/target ownership; backfill existing bill lines as allocations without changing historical values or IDs. Add a new migration, never edit historical migrations. Rebuild the unique-order constraint safely with all dependent foreign keys and immutability triggers preserved; transactionally run foreign-key and integrity checks and reject inconsistent legacy data rather than guess. Update upgrade, backup/restore and version gates. Implement transactional selection, replay identity, reservation/resume, remaining-only correction/movement, and conditional closure together. Keep split creation unreachable through production IPC/UI until every mutation and read path is safe with multiple bills. Existing full-bill behavior must keep working during migration and against legacy unpaid and paid bills. Direct trusted-code tests must cover failures and concurrent writers before the gate opens.
4. **Complete waiter experience and activation.** Add item-unit selection and distinct reserved/available displays to the billing owner in English and Albanian, with accessible keyboard and supported layouts; maintain full-bill flow. Wire only narrow owner commands and retain write exclusion, request lifetimes and identity cleanup. Provide durable resume and reconciliation of the exact bill/payment after restart; do not rely solely on component state. Refresh the still-open order after a partial payment and hand off only where appropriate. Activate the flow only together with the service, migration, report, correction, move and recovery protections above. Review renderer ownership and the actual contract if composition changes; never relax the architecture gate to pass.
5. **End-to-end verification and delivery.** Independently review and test the combined change, fix findings and recheck affected paths. Reconcile repeated splits and final payment, later sends, unpaid restart/resume, back before commitment, stale and simultaneous requests, denied direct calls, payment/print uncertainty and retry, account corrections, movement and day/shift changes. Verify historical migration, recovery, print jobs, restricted visibility and report snapshots. Record automated, rendered desktop, offline and physical-printer evidence separately; do not infer physical output from queue acceptance. Keep S-094 In progress until its full acceptance and PR workflow are complete.

Each slice must leave the application safe to use with its existing sample workflow. A slice that cannot satisfy that condition stays unexposed until integrated; this plan does not authorize an incomplete split-payment path. Final implementation may adjust internal representation after review, but it must preserve the invariants and acceptance above.
